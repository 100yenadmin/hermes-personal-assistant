# Connectivity research worker contract

Objective: bounded [lane] evidence for [countries], [dates/duration].
Context: minimal nonidentifying device/workload constraints; separate known from unknown.
Route: actual approved provider/model/effort; no silent paid fallback.
Authority: read-only public research. No purchases/activation/contact, logins/cookie extraction, customer writes or global routing changes.
Sources: agent-reach official discovery; social lane also last30days and Reddit routing. As-of [aware ISO]; Reddit cutoff [six CALENDAR months], reject older/future/undated.
Scope: nonoverlapping named product/category list, bounded search/time budget. Stop with explicit incomplete evidence instead of looping.
Output: isolated JSON claim/option records plus Markdown. Country/plan/format/duration/network; currency/total/fees; phone and hotspot allowances/reset/throttle/FUP; down/up/latency with evidence type; compatibility/logistics; URL, published_at if social, retrieved_at, decisive quote and unresolved questions.
Coverage: attempted/success/empty/blocked and exclusion reasons. No exhaustive-market or independent-benchmark claims. Referrals have zero ranking weight. No cross-product/country policy transfer.
Parent owns recommendation, verification and canonical state. Return compact summary and absolute artifact paths.
