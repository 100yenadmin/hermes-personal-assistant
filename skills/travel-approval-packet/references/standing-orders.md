# Standing Orders

Use existing profile credential and browser integrations plus any already configured payment method to reach the final button. No specific wallet vendor is required. Ask once with a final-state summary before final booking, payment, cancellation, check-in, order, ride, reservation, outbound contact, material profile or payment change, unclear terms, deposit, funding, or other paid or irreversible submit.

User budgets are delegated authority to reach the final button, not permission to skip the final-state confirmation. Denial, timeout, or changed details means do not proceed.
