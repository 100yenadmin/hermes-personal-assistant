# Local dining sources and query recipes

These are public entry points and search strategies, not proof of a venue's
current award, hours, prices or booking availability. Replace example years with
the destination-local year being researched; a year in a query never dates the
returned review or photo. Open the exact matched
branch and inspect the page on each run. Do not scrape behind logins, sign up,
or use Instagram login; public posts are optional evidence, never a dependency.

## Japan

- Curate: [Michelin Guide](https://guide.michelin.com/jp/ja/), then
  [Tabelog Award](https://award.tabelog.com/) and
  [Hyakumeiten](https://award.tabelog.com/hyakumeiten/).
- Local reviews/photos/menu: [Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/), with visit and
  photo dates where readable; the review posting date need not be visit date.
- Queries: `難波 ビブグルマン site:guide.michelin.com`,
  `なんば ミシュラン ビブグルマン 火曜日 夜`,
  `<店名 支店> 食べログ メニュー 写真 2026`,
  `<店名> 定休日 営業時間 ラストオーダー 臨時休業 公式`.
- Do not confuse Osaka-wide results with Namba. Before expanding, enumerate
  every entry on the official category list across all pages; inspect its exact
  address/area and retain each local match as a pick, an exclusion or an
  unverified lead. A nearby venue with conflicting hours must not disappear
  from the accounting. Save declared total / checked count and any coverage gap.
  State a wider search radius explicitly; use map directions for distance/time,
  not guesswork. A former Bib Gourmand in a blog may now be Selected or absent;
  check the Guide itself.
- Reservations use `personal-assistant:restaurant-reservations` and its
  `references/japan-korea.md` (Tabelog, TableCheck and other provider routes).

## Korea

- Curate: [Blue Ribbon Survey](https://www.bluer.co.kr/) and the
  [Michelin Guide](https://guide.michelin.com/kr/ko/). Ribbon count and edition
  must come from the matched listing, not a marketing badge with no year.
- Local platforms: [Naver Map](https://map.naver.com/),
  [Naver Place](https://m.place.naver.com/) and
  [Kakao Map](https://map.kakao.com/). Search in Korean even when writing an
  English answer. Try publicly indexed branch pages and the public Blue Ribbon
  listing if a dynamic map cannot be extracted; report the limitation.
- Queries: `서울 <동네> 블루리본 맛집`, `송도 <음식> 네이버 지도`,
  `<상호 지점> site:bluer.co.kr`, `<상호> site:place.map.kakao.com`,
  `<상호> site:m.place.naver.com`, `<상호> 메뉴 가격 영업시간 화요일 휴무`,
  `<상호> 2026 방문 후기 사진 브레이크타임 라스트오더`.
- Same-named branches and relocated main stores are a known identity trap.
  Match Korean address and phone; do not attach old-branch photos or hours to
  a new address. Treat queue entry as an outbound action, not research.
- Use reservations' Japan/Korea reference for CATCHTABLE/Naver/app/phone needs;
  reading a map listing does not establish that an overseas visitor can book.

## Taiwan

- Curate: [Michelin Guide Taiwan](https://guide.michelin.com/tw/zh_TW/), then
  local media such as [TVBS 食尚玩家](https://supertaste.tvbs.com.tw/).
  A local area without a verified Guide match is not a license to invent one.
- Local listings: [Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps) plus
  [iFoodie 愛食記](https://ifoodie.tw/). Follow to dated original articles for
  photos/menu, rather than treating a roundup update date as a photo date.
- Queries: `羅東 羊肉爐 菜單 2026`, `羅東 羊肉 火鍋 星期二 晚餐`,
  `<店名 羅東店> 營業時間 週二 公休 最後點餐`,
  `<店名> 現金 服務費 用餐時間 訂位`, `<店名> site:ifoodie.tw`.
- Lamb/mutton searches should try `羊肉`, `羊肉爐`, `羊排`; preserve what the
  menu actually calls the meat rather than asserting a sheep's age or origin.
- Branch-specific prices beat chain-wide roundups. A photo of a menu board
  documents that board at its stated date, not today's guaranteed bill.

## Elsewhere or blocked platforms

Use the same hierarchy: current local awards → locally used review/map platform
in the local language → exact official venue menu/notices → dated independent
visits. Inspect the public page through an already available read-only browser
when extraction fails. Never install a browser, acquire credentials or change
runtime configuration merely to complete discovery. A blocked platform is a
coverage gap, not permission to fabricate a rating, an image date or a booking
route. Cite search snippets only as snippets and never as a read full review.
