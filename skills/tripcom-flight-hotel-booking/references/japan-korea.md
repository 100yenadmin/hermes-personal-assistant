A partner redirect must be named and checked as the actual seller rather than treated as the airline/hotel's own checkout.
If the selected route needs the user's login, domestic SMS or app, preserve flight numbers/property, dates, guests, fare/room, extras and terms. Give exact steps to the same option and review page; the user handles verification. Ask for explicit yes before final submit, change or cancellation, including any cancel-and-rebook sequence.
