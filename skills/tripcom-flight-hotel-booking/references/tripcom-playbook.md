# Trip.com Playbook

Start at `https://www.trip.com/` and continue only on a Trip.com-owned domain.

For flights: enter origin, destination, dates, travelers, cabin, and stops. Compare at least three options when available. Normalize taxes, baggage, seat fees, and payment fees. Prefer options matching the user's constraints over cheaper options that violate bags, stops, timing, or refund preferences.

For hotels: enter city or meeting location, dates, guests, room count, and budget. Compare total stay price, taxes, resort fees, cancellation window, location, room type, bed type, and check-in time.

Treat "book if under X" as delegated authority to select, fill, and reach the final button, not permission to skip the final confirmation. Ask again if provider price, dates, traveler, refund terms, payment descriptor, or final button label changes after confirmation.

Use the profile's existing credential and browser integrations plus an already configured payment method to reach the final button. Enter missing checkout details that the user has delegated or that are available in the profile. Ask before material profile or payment changes, identity upload, or final paid submit.
