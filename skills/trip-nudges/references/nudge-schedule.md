# Nudge Schedule

Default triggers:

- T-24h check-in window.
- T-12h weather and packing.
- T-4h baggage and documents.
- T-3h domestic or T-4h international leave-for-airport, adjusted by traffic when available.
- Arrival plus 30 minutes hotel/food/local prompt.

Avoid non-urgent nudges between 10 PM and 7 AM local user time. Suppress nudges when itinerary facts are stale, contradicted, missing, or unverifiable.
